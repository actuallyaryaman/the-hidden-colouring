Take the evidence with you.

Download the public record. It contains every completed round’s commitments, challenge and two openings—never unopened answers or session credentials.

Verify it from a separate terminal, even after the server has stopped:

python3 transcript.py transcript.json

A saved record proves its openings are consistent. It cannot prove the challenges were unpredictable or establish that a graph has a solution. That distinction is why an arranged transcript is not a successful live proof.
